WHO WE ARE

Making Medicine Information Easier to Explore

MedInfo Portal is an educational medicine information website designed to help users explore basic information about commonly known medicines.

The portal provides information such as medicine names, generic names, categories, common uses, general precautions, possible side effects and storage information.

Our goal is to present information in a simple, organized and user-friendly way.
