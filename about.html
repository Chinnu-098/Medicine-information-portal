About Medicine Information Portal

Making medicine-related information simple and accessible.

What is this Portal?

The Medicine Information Portal is a web-based educational application designed to provide organized and easy-to-understand information about medicines.

Users can search for medicines and view useful information such as their common uses, dosage guidance, possible side effects, precautions and storage instructions.

The portal is designed to be useful for students, healthcare learners and the general public who want to learn basic information about medicines.

Project Objectives

Main goals of the Medicine Information Portal

Easy Search

Provide a simple search interface that allows users to find medicine information quickly.

Educational Information

Present medicine-related information in a clear and student-friendly format.

Responsive Design

Make the website accessible on desktops, tablets and mobile devices.
